Show Notes

In this episode of The Fixers Podcast, host Amrita Kumar talks with Søren Hermansen, manager of the Energy Academy on the Danish island of Samsø, about how a community of 3,500 people became one of the world's leading models for fossil-fuel independence.

We explore how Samsø made the shift starting in 1998—moving from wind turbines and district heating to a broader focus on land use and biodiversity—and why Søren found that leading with "why" mattered more than leading with "how." The conversation moves outward from Samsø to rural communities in the U.S., including an unexpected case study of a meditation college thriving in the middle of Iowa farmland, and closes with Søren's take on whether a world built around dense, high-consumption cities could ever run on renewables alone.

Whether you're drawn to the policy side of climate action or want to understand what it really takes to move a community toward change, this conversation will change how you think about sustainability, trust, and leadership.

Learn more about the Energy Academy Samsø at energiakademiet.dk

The Fixers Podcast

Fixers is a podcast about real people solving real problems. Hosted by high schooler Amrita Kumar, each episode features entrepreneurs, inventors, and changemakers tackling challenges around the world—from cleaning up oceans to empowering farmers. With curiosity and a drive to understand how things actually get fixed, Amrita digs into what these fixers do, how they do it, and what we can learn from their work. Fixers is for anyone who believes the world's biggest problems are worth solving—and wants to know where to start.
